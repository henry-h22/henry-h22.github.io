abstract: >-
  This project explored the production of tone in White Hmong, an asian language spoken in Southeast Asia, as well as in the Twin Cities in Minnesota. Working alongside Zuoyu Tian (Macalester College), I used generalized additive models and neural networks to probe the effects of contextual and semantic factors on the realization of pitch within the low-rising tone. Presented at the first ever Macalester Linguistics Department Student Colloqium.
